Wye Valley Meadery

Wye Valley Meadery was founded in 2018 by brothers Matt and Kit Newell in the Welsh Borders. The producer now trades as Hive Mind Mead & Brew Co., making traditional and modern sparkling Welsh Meads with British honey, including honey from its own bees.
