The Rookery is a small craft mead producer based in Perthshire, Scotland. Its [official mead page](https://www.rookery.scot/about-our-mead) says honey provides the majority of fermentables in every recipe and that it does not substitute sugar or syrup for that core ingredient.

Its changing range draws on seasonal and often foraged ingredients, including heather, meadowsweet, rowan berries, crab apples and hedgerow fruit. These are modern meads inspired by Scottish plants and archaeological evidence rather than one fixed “ancient” recipe.

## Availability

Rookery bottles appear through independent shops and event sellers, but individual flavours can be scarce or sold out. A third-party shop may retain a page long after a batch has gone. Check the producer’s current website or social channels before making a special journey or ordering from an unfamiliar listing.

The producer’s live site and Perthshire trading address were verified on **11 October 2026**, but this does not guarantee that every named flavour is currently available.
