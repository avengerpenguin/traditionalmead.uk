Title: Raven Forge Viking Mead
Summary: Raven Forge Viking mead guide covering BloodMoon, its Broadland Drinks attribution, British production context and current buying information.

Raven Forge is a British retailer and Viking-inspired lifestyle brand whose range includes themed blades, drinking vessels, gifts and mead. Its consumer-facing mead presentation centres on Norse legends and celebration rather than claiming to reproduce a documented historical recipe.

## Who makes Raven Forge mead?

Raven Forge describes BloodMoon as “Raven Forge's Viking-inspired mead”, but its official Shopify product data identifies **Broadland Drinks** as both vendor and structured-data brand. Broadland Drinks operates from The New Winery in Cawston, Norfolk, and says it develops, ferments and produces British-made drinks.

This supports describing BloodMoon as a Raven Forge-branded product for which Broadland is the identified vendor and brand, consistent with Broadland being the supplier or production partner. Raven Forge should not be presented as the confirmed physical mead producer, and no Broadland product page naming the exact production arrangement was available when checked in October 2026.

## Raven Forge meads reviewed

- [[BloodMoon Viking Mead]] — a 14.5% ABV honey-and-black-cherry-flavoured mead with sweet honey, tangy dark fruit and a broody red appearance.

Raven Forge's current BloodMoon page says the drink is made using 100% renewable energy. Its 70cl bottle and Ragnarök story make it strongly gift-oriented, although promotional extras such as a drinking horn should be checked against the live offer rather than assumed to be standard.
