Wye Valley Meadery was founded in 2018 by brothers Matt and Kit Newell in the Welsh Borders. The producer now trades as [Hive Mind Mead & Brew Co.](https://hivemindmead.com/), making traditional and modern sparkling [[Welsh Meads]] with British honey, including honey from its own bees.
