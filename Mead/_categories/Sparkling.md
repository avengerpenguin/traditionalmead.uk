Sparkling mead uses carbonation to give fermented honey a lighter, livelier texture. It ranges from wine-strength bottles to modern session meads designed to be served chilled like cider, beer or a botanical spritz.

Carbonation can temper perceived sweetness and lift floral or fruit aromas. Always check the label for strength and serving size: sparkling does not necessarily mean low alcohol.
